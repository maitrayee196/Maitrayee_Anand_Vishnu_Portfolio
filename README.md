## Publications

**State-Dependent Transmission of Long-Term Yields to Real Economic Activity and Inflation: A VAR Analysis Across Two Monetary Regimes**
A Vector Autoregression study of how 10 Year Treasury yield shocks transmit to GDP and CPI across two monetary regimes. Finds transmission is state dependent, with the same yield shock producing responses 1.16x to 1.82x larger and three months faster after COVID.
[Read on SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6884958)

**Government Debt, IMF Programs, and Macroeconomic Performance**
A panel study of 4,109 country year observations across 124 countries (1990 to 2024), progressing from pooled OLS to Two Way Fixed Effects Difference in Differences to reach causally credible estimates.
[Read on SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6748542)
