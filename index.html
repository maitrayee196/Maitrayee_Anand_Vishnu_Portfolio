Risk · Capital Planning

GSIB Liquidity Stress Testing Console

Problem
How resilient is the U.S. banking system's liquidity to a sharp funding shock, and which banks are most exposed?
Analysis
Built a stress testing model for the six U.S. GSIBs, sourcing HQLA and net cash outflows from SEC quarterly filings and Pillar 3 LCR disclosures; computed stressed LCR, survival horizon, and dollar shortfall across 4 macro scenarios (2022 rate cycle, 2008 GFC, 2023 SVB).
Recommendation
Prioritize liquidity buffer monitoring on the 4 banks that breach the Basel III minimum under moderate stress; size contingency funding to the modeled dollar shortfall.
Business impact
A moderate 2022 magnitude stress pushes 4 of 6 banks below the 100% Basel III LCR minimum, a clear early warning signal for capital and funding decisions.

Restructuring · Distressed

Spirit Airlines "Chapter 22" Restructuring Model

Problem
When a company reenters bankruptcy, how much do creditors actually recover, and can the cash runway be modeled in real time from public filings?
Analysis
Built a restructuring model of Spirit's 2025 repeat Chapter 11 from SEC filings and court documents, including a 13 week cash flow, creditor recovery waterfall under the Absolute Priority Rule, and wind down valuation, with modeled crisis events (collateral demands, lease terminations).
Recommendation
Use the 13 week cash bridge to flag the defensive revolver draw and covenant pressure points; frame recovery expectations by creditor class before negotiations.
Business impact
Calibrated within $7M of actual reported cash and correctly reproduced the timing of Spirit's defensive revolver draw, demonstrating a reliable distressed monitoring framework.

Macro · Planning Inputs

Energy Inflation Correlation Engine

Problem
How much, and how quickly, do oil price moves feed into consumer inflation, and which inflation measure should planners actually track?
Analysis
Quantified oil price pass through into U.S. inflation using live FRED data (2000 to 2026) and time lagged OLS regressions across headline, core, and gasoline CPI.
Recommendation
Anchor forecasting and hedging assumptions to core CPI for signal stability; treat headline CPI as a fast but noisy oil driven input.
Business impact
Oil explains roughly 77% of gasoline CPI and 40% of headline CPI within one month, while core responds weakly (about 10%) over roughly 10 months, validating why the Fed, and planners, track core.

Published Research · SSRN
SSRN · Managerial Economics · May 2026

State-Dependent Transmission of Long-Term Yields to Real Economic Activity and Inflation: A VAR Analysis Across Two Monetary Regimes

A Vector Autoregression study of how 10 Year Treasury yield shocks transmit to GDP and CPI across the 2014 to 2019 normalization and 2021 to 2025 tightening cycles. The central finding is that transmission is state dependent: the same 100bp yield shock produces GDP and CPI responses 1.16× to 1.82× larger and three months faster after COVID, with CPI now 38% yield driven at the 12 month horizon (up from 15%).

SSRN · Econometrics · May 2026

Government Debt, IMF Programs, and Macroeconomic Performance

A panel study of 4,109 country year observations across 124 countries (1990 to 2024), progressing from pooled OLS to Two Way Fixed Effects Difference in Differences. Finds government debt strongly and negatively associated with GDP growth (β = 0.0159 lower growth per pp of debt/GDP, p<0.001), IMF participation linked to roughly 0.50 to 0.55pp lower annual growth, and financial development as the dominant anti inflationary structural force.
